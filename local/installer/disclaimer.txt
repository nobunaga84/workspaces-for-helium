HELIUM WORKSPACES -- PLEASE READ

This is an unofficial personal add-on for Helium (https://github.com/imputnet/helium).
It is an AI-assisted ("vibe-coded") patch set that I made for my own use. It is not
affiliated with or endorsed by the Helium team, and I have no intention of submitting
it to official Helium. Do not report its bugs to them.

If you miss Arc's Spaces, you are welcome to use it as it is.

WHAT THIS INSTALLS

- Helium with Arc-style workspaces, in a folder of its own:
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Helium Workspaces
- a profile of its own, made the first time you open it:
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Helium Workspaces\User Data
- a Start-menu shortcut, an entry in Apps & features, and an entry in
  Settings > Apps > Default apps (nothing changes until you pick it there).

It does not touch an installed official Helium or its profile.

IT NEVER UPDATES ITSELF

There is no updater. Security fixes arrive only when a new version of this installer
is built on a newer Helium, and that can lag behind official Helium. Workspaces can
break after an update.

TWO THINGS TO KNOW

- To make it your default browser, use Windows Settings > Apps > Default apps >
  Workspaces for Helium. Its own settings page cannot do it.
- Pin it to the taskbar from the Start menu (right-click > Pin to taskbar).
  A pin made before it was called Workspaces for Helium keeps working; unpin and pin it
  again to show the new name.

Uninstalling keeps your data unless you tick "Also delete my Workspaces for Helium data".
